The Idea

How the Society works

One buy‑in, one bracket, one night at the table. Here's the loop every player runs.

01

Create your account

Sign up with your name, phone and a photo — so the table knows who you are, and the Society can track your ranking across every event.

02

Pick an event, pay in

Browse open tournaments around Lebanon and lock your seat with a fixed entrance fee, e.g. $25.

03

Show up and play

Matches happen in person at the venue. Win your board, advance to the next round of the bracket.

04

Climb, win, repeat

The top 3 finishers win cash prizes. Every result also moves you up the Society‑wide rankings.

Why We Built This

Backgammon belongs at the table, not the app.

Lebanon has always played — on balconies, in cafés, between rounds of coffee. The Lebanon Backgammon Society turns that into a real circuit: entrance fees that fund real prize pools, brackets that mean something, and a ranking that follows you from Achrafieh to Byblos.

We're not replacing the board with a screen. The app just handles the parts that used to be a headache — who's in, who you're playing, what's at stake — so the table can do the rest.
