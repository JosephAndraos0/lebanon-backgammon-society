How the Society works

One entry fee, one bracket, one night at the table. That's the whole idea.

01

Create your profile

Add your name, phone number and a photo. It's how the table knows who you are, and how we keep score for you all season.

02

Pick a tournament, pay in

Browse open tournaments around Lebanon and lock in your seat with a flat entry fee, like $25.

03

Show up and play

Matches happen in person, at the venue, best of three games. Win two, move on to the next round.

04

Win, climb the ranks

Every result counts toward the season leaderboard. Prizes for each tournament are announced by the organizer.

Why We Started This

Backgammon belongs at the table, not the app.

People have always played backgammon here: on balconies, in cafés, between rounds of coffee. We just gave it some structure. Real entry fees, a bracket that means something, and a leaderboard that follows you from Achrafieh to Byblos.

We're not replacing the board with a screen. The app just handles the parts that used to be a headache: who's in, who you're playing, what's at stake. The table does the rest.

Terms of Service

Last updated: September 2026

1. Who we are

Lebanon Backgammon Society is a small, independently run project that organizes in-person backgammon tournaments in Lebanon. We're not a registered company — think of us as an organizer with a website. These terms exist so everyone, players and us, knows where they stand.

2. Using the site

You need an account to join a tournament. When you sign up, we ask for your real name, phone number, and a photo — that's so other players and the organizer know who's actually going to be at the table. Give accurate information; don't create an account for someone else or impersonate another player.

3. Entering a tournament

Entry fees are shown up front on each tournament's page. Your seat is reserved for a short window while you pay; if payment doesn't go through in time, the seat goes back up for grabs. Once your payment clears, your seat is confirmed by our payment processor, not just by your browser telling us so.

4. Paying for a friend

You can pay for a friend's seat in the same checkout. They'll get a link by email to claim it, or if they already have an account under that email, they'll be offered it the next time they sign in. If they turn it down, their seat opens back up; refunding you for that seat is handled manually by the organizer, not automatically.

5. Prizes

We don't award cash prizes. Any prizes for a tournament are decided and announced by the organizer, at the event or later, and may change from one tournament to the next. Entry fees are not a prize pool.

6. Matches, brackets, and disputes

Brackets are seeded automatically once entry closes. Match results are entered by the organizer, and the organizer's decision on a result is final. We reserve the right to remove a player from an event, with a refund of unplayed entry fees at our discretion, for disruptive behavior, cheating, or no-shows.

7. Refunds

Entry fees are generally non-refundable once a tournament's bracket has been built, except at the organizer's discretion, for example if an event is cancelled. If you believe you were charged in error, contact us and we'll review it.

8. No guarantees

This is a community-run project, not a professional service. We do our best to keep the site working and tournaments running smoothly, but we can't promise the site will always be available or bug-free. We're not liable for indirect losses, like travel costs or missed work, related to a tournament being delayed, changed, or cancelled.

9. Changes to these terms

We may update these terms as the project grows. If we do, we'll update the date at the top. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the new terms.

10. Contact

Questions about these terms? Email lebanonbackgammonsociety@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. What we collect

When you create an account: your name, email address, and password, handled by our authentication provider, Supabase — we never see or store your raw password.

When you complete your profile: your phone number, a profile photo, and your self-rated skill level.

When you pay for a tournament: your payment is handled entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number — Stripe only tells us whether the payment succeeded.

If you invite a friend: their name and email address, so we can send them a claim link.

2. How we use it

Your name and photo are shown publicly on the site, on event pages, brackets, and the leaderboard, so other players know who they're playing. Your phone number and email are private — only the organizer can see them, to reach you about a tournament: a seat, a schedule change, a result.

If you opted in to updates, we may email you about upcoming tournaments. You can opt out any time by telling us.

3. Where it's stored

Your data lives in our database and file storage, provided by Supabase. Payments are processed by Stripe. Emails (account confirmation, password resets, friend invites) are sent through Resend. We picked these because they're standard, reputable providers — we don't sell or share your data with anyone else.

4. Cookies and tracking

We don't run ads, and we don't use tracking or analytics scripts. The site stores your login session in your browser so you don't have to sign in every time — that's it.

5. Your choices

You can update your name, phone number, and photo any time from your profile. To have your account and data deleted, email us — we'll remove what we can, though we may need to keep basic records of a paid tournament, for example that an entry fee was received, for our own bookkeeping.

6. Who this is for

This site is for people old enough to enter a paid tournament and hold a valid payment card. It isn't intended for children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle your data, we'll update this page and the date above.

8. Contact

Questions about your data? Email lebanonbackgammonsociety@gmail.com.
