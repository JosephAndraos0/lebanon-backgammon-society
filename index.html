The Idea

How the Society works

One entry fee, one bracket, one night at the table. That's the whole idea.

01

Create your profile

Add your name, phone number and a photo. It's how the table knows who you are, and how we keep score for you all season.

02

Pick a tournament, pay in

Browse open tournaments around Lebanon and lock in your seat with a flat entry fee, like $25.

03

Show up and play

Matches happen in person, at the venue. Win your board, move on to the next round.

04

Win, get paid

The top three finishers split real prize money. Every result also counts toward the season leaderboard.

Why We Started This

Backgammon belongs at the table, not the app.

People have always played backgammon here: on balconies, in cafés, between rounds of coffee. We just gave it some structure. Real entry fees, real prize money, a bracket that means something, and a leaderboard that follows you from Achrafieh to Byblos.

We're not replacing the board with a screen. The app just handles the parts that used to be a headache: who's in, who you're playing, what's at stake. The table does the rest.
